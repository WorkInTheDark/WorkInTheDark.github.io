I study how humans and AI agents collaborate effectively, and how their partnership unlocks new ways of thinking, working, and creating together.

Bingsheng Arthur Yao

Associate Research Scientist, Khoury College of Computer Sciences, Northeastern University

My research investigates how people collaborate, and what it takes for AI agents to become effective partners in human work. By studying collaborative processes and designing human–AI systems, I envision a future of collaborative symbiosis, where humans and AI agents complement one another to expand what we can accomplish together.

Previously a postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Dakuo Wang. PhD from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Jim Hendler.

Recruiting. I am actively seeking self-motivated research assistants. See the Prospective Students page for the application form and FAQs.
